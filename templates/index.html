Entry opportunities ENTRY SIGNALS

Gap detector OVERNIGHT
A gap is the distance between today's open and yesterday's close — price that never traded. What matters is less the size than whether it has been filled: an unfilled gap leaves an untested level behind, a filled one has already been rejected intraday. Size is also shown in ATR, because 2% is routine in one stock and a shock in another.
Backtest SAME RULES, ON HISTORY
The engine replays exactly the rules it gives you today — the same entry signals, the same initial stop, the same move to breakeven and the same trailing stop — bar by bar over the stock's history. No lookahead: a signal detected at a close is entered at the next session's open.
